# Customer Status Response Scripts
## What to Tell Customers Based on Their Account Status

---

## 1. DEPOSIT NEEDED

**Situation:** Customer has activated but hasn't paid all required deposits.

### Script:
"Okay, so I'm showing that you've activated your certificate but you still need to pay your deposit to move forward.

Your total deposit is **$[TOTAL_EXPECTED]**. So far you've paid **$[TOTAL_PAID]**, so you have **$[REMAINING]** remaining.

**If activated online:**
You can pay your deposit at **activatemytrip.com** using the same login you created when you activated.

**If activated by mail:**
You can mail a check or money order payable to **Casablanca Express** to:

**2248 Townsgate Road, Unit 1
Westlake Village, California 91361**

Be sure to include your certificate number on the check.

Once your deposit is received, you'll be able to schedule your travel dates. Is there anything else I can help you with?"

---

## 2. READY TO SCHEDULE

**Situation:** Deposits are complete, customer needs to pick travel dates.

### Script:
"Great news! Your deposits are all paid, so you're ready to schedule your travel dates.

We're currently booking into **[CURRENT BOOKING WINDOW - e.g., May and June]** for most trips. Some destinations book further out.

When you call to schedule, please have:
- Your certificate number ready
- A calendar available
- Flexibility on dates if possible

Would you like me to transfer you to our scheduling department right now? They're available Monday through Friday, 9am to 5pm Pacific.

**[If yes]** One moment while I transfer you.

**[If no]** No problem! When you're ready, call us at **818-992-5100** and press 1 for scheduling."

---

## 3. DATES SCHEDULED

**Situation:** Deposits paid, travel dates selected, but more than 75 days out (no TR assigned yet).

### Script:
"I'm showing your trip is all set! Your travel dates are confirmed for **[TRAVEL_DATE]**, which is **[DAYS_UNTIL_TRAVEL]** days away.

Here's what happens next:

A travel representative will be assigned to your account **45 to 75 days before your trip**. They'll call you to go over all the details of your promotional package and confirm your traveler information.

You don't need to do anything right now - just make sure to answer calls from the **805 area code** when it gets closer to your trip.

Is there anything else I can help you with today?"

---

## 4. WAITING FOR TRAVEL REP

**Situation:** Travel date is 1-75 days away, no TR assigned yet (urgent).

### Script:
"I see your trip is coming up on **[TRAVEL_DATE]** - that's only **[DAYS_UNTIL_TRAVEL]** days away!

You should have a travel representative assigned to your account by now. Let me make a note to have someone reach out to you right away.

Can you confirm the best phone number to reach you? **[CONFIRM PHONE]**

Please make sure to answer calls from the **805 area code** - that will be your travel rep calling.

If you don't hear from someone within **24-48 hours**, please call us back at **818-992-5100** and let them know you need a travel rep assigned urgently.

Is there anything else I can help you with?"

---

## 5. TRAVEL REP ASSIGNED

**Situation:** TR assigned but hasn't completed the confirmation call yet.

### Script:
"I'm showing that **[TR_NAME]** has been assigned as your travel representative.

They should be reaching out to you soon to go over all the details of your trip and confirm your traveler information.

Please make sure to answer calls from the **805 area code** - that's where our travel reps call from.

If you need to reach them directly, you can call **818-992-5100** and ask to speak with **[TR_NAME]** or leave a message for them.

Is there anything else I can help you with?"

---

## 6. BOOKING PENDING

**Situation:** Customer spoke with TR, made travel decisions, waiting for hotel/flight booking.

### Script:
"I see you've already spoken with your travel representative and made your travel decisions - great!

Right now your booking is being processed. A booking agent will contact you **7 to 14 days before your trip** with your exact flight times and hotel confirmation.

Your itinerary and all confirmation details will be emailed to you once everything is finalized.

Just sit tight - you're almost there! Is there anything else I can help you with?"

---

## 7. TRAVEL PENDING

**Situation:** Everything is booked, trip is confirmed, just waiting to travel.

### Script:
"Exciting news - your trip is all booked and confirmed!

You're headed to **[DESTINATION]** on **[ARRIVAL_DATE]** - that's just **[DAYS_UNTIL_ARRIVAL]** days away!

Your itinerary and confirmation details should have been emailed to you. If you haven't received them or need them resent, I can make a note to have those sent again.

**Before you travel, remember:**
- Bring valid government-issued ID for all travelers
- Arrive at the airport at least 2 hours before your flight
- You're responsible for ground transportation to/from the airport and hotel

Have a wonderful trip! Is there anything else you need before you go?"

---

## 8. TRIP COMPLETE

**Situation:** Customer has already traveled.

### Script:
"I see you've already taken your trip to **[DESTINATION]** - welcome back! I hope you had a wonderful time.

Is there something I can help you with today?

**[If they have questions/issues:]**
Let me see what I can do to help with that...

**[If they want another trip:]**
That's great! We do have some special offers available. Would you like me to transfer you to someone who can tell you about our current promotions?"

---

## 9. REFUND PENDING

**Situation:** Customer has a refund being processed.

### Script:
"I see there's a refund of **$[REFUND_AMOUNT]** being processed on your account.

Refunds typically take **4 to 6 weeks** to process from the date they were initiated. The refund will be sent to the address we have on file.

**[If docs not mailed yet:]**
The paperwork is still being processed. Once it's complete, you'll receive documentation in the mail.

**[If docs already mailed:]**
The refund documentation was mailed on **[MAIL_DATE]**. If you haven't received it, please allow a few more days for delivery.

If you have questions about your refund status, you can call our customer service line at **818-992-5100**, Monday through Friday, 9am to 5pm Pacific.

Is there anything else I can help you with?"
