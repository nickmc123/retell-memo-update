# Booking Disclosure Script
## Read to Customer When Scheduling Travel Dates

---

### DISCLOSURE (Read Verbatim)

Before I confirm your dates, I need to go over a few important things about your promotional trip:

**Flight Times:**
Because this is a promotional package using group travel fares, flights tend to depart between 1pm and 7pm on your departure day, and return flights can leave as early as 9am on your return day. Flights may include stopovers, layovers, or plane changes.

**Accommodations:**
The promotional hotels are economy class - clean and safe, but basic. They are not located near the beach or main attractions.

**Ground Transportation:**
You are responsible for all ground transportation to and from the airport and hotel.

**Changes and Cancellations:**
Once dates are confirmed, changes are subject to availability and may incur fees. Cancellations follow the terms on your certificate.

**Do you understand and accept these terms?**

[Wait for confirmation]

Great! Your travel dates are now confirmed for [DATE]. A travel representative will contact you 45-75 days before your trip to finalize the details.

**"Can I get better flights?"**
> We do offer upgrades that remove you from group travel and give you better flight times. Would you like me to transfer you to someone who can discuss upgrade options?

**"Can I get a better hotel?"**
> Upgrades also include better hotel options. I can transfer you to discuss that if you'd like.

**"What if I need to change dates later?"**
> Date changes are possible based on availability. Call us at 818-992-5100 at least 30 days before your trip to request changes.
