## 💰 Budget & Bidding

### Recommended Starting Budget
- **Daily Budget**: $50-100/day
- **Campaign Type**: Lead Generation
- **Bidding Strategy**: Lowest Cost Per Result (initially)
- **After 100 leads**: Switch to Cost Cap ($15-25 per lead)

### Expected Performance
- **CPM**: $8-15
- **CTR**: 1.5-3%
- **Cost Per Lead**: $10-30 (varies by audience)
- **Lead-to-Call Conversion**: 100% (instant calling)
- **Call-to-Sale Conversion**: 10-25% (depends on Brianna's performance)

---

## 📊 Testing Strategy

### Week 1: Creative Test
Run all 3 ad options with equal budget, see which performs best

### Week 2: Audience Test
Test the 3 core audiences with winning creative

### Week 3: Scale
Double budget on best-performing creative + audience combo

### Ongoing: Optimization
- Pause ads with cost-per-lead > $30
- Scale ads with cost-per-lead < $15
- Refresh creative every 2 weeks (ad fatigue)
